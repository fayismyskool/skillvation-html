Faculty Development Program (FDP)

Description

This Faculty Development Programme (FDP) is a structured 2-day intensive training designed for educators to upgrade their teaching methodologies using modern 3D and AI-powered platforms. The programme focuses on: Practical 3D & AI classroom integration Interactive lesson planning Technology-based assessment methods Hands-on practice sessions Academic content enhancement strategies Participants will receive guided training, live demonstrations, and practice modules to effectively implement AI-enabled tools in Science, Mathematics, and Social Studies classrooms. Mode: Online / Offline Includes: Free 3D/AI Practice Sessions Certification: Completion Certificate Provided
